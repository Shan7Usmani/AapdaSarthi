# AapdaSarthi — One-Page Executive Summary

> **Flood Response Copilot** · a two-sided resource-allocation platform that connects
> **citizens in distress** → **nearest rescuer** → **HQ command center** — live, offline-capable, realtime.
> Live product: **https://production-inky-eight.vercel.app** · Team: FALCONX · DecodeSIH 2026 · BHARAT SHAKTI · PS3

---

## 1. The Problem
When floods hit, the **first 15 minutes decide who lives** — but emergency response is
overwhelmed, citizens have **no way to reach help**, and headquarters **flies blind**.
The killer is *misallocation*: rescuers, boats, and medkits exist in the same city as the
people in the water — they just **can't find each other**.

## 2. The Solution
**Every citizen = a sensor · every rescuer = a node · headquarters = an AI command center.**
AapdaSarthi routes help to the **nearest available rescuer**, ranked by **risk**, in real time — so
the right resource reaches the right person in minutes, not hours.

1. **Citizen SOS** — one-tap distress with GPS + photo + voice. **Works with zero internet** (local save + SMS fallback).
2. **Rescuer** — nearest-team dispatch, distance-sorted, "take control" of a response.
3. **HQ Command** — live rainfall map, **AI risk engine** (who's in danger first), multilingual alerts, realtime board.

## 3. Why We Win (Traction)
- **It's live, not a mockup.** Deployed, running product with realistic flows already built.
- **Offline-first.** SOS saves locally → auto-syncs when signal returns; cellular **SMS fallback** to emergency number. Built for a disaster zone, not a demo room.
- **Realtime.** Every SOS appears on the HQ board across devices instantly.
- **Near-zero cost at scale.** Built entirely on free tiers (Open-Meteo data, Vercel, Supabase) — deployable to a new district in days.

## 4. Impact & Scale Path
- **Who it reaches:** NDRF & state disaster authorities + millions of at-risk citizens.
- **Path:** one city → river basin → nation. Free for citizens; SaaS seat for agencies.

## 5. Ask
We want to **partner with a live disaster authority** to pilot AapdaSarthi in the next flood season. Roadmap: AI dispatch, mesh/satellite fallback, and rain-forecast fusion — turning warnings from hours into days.
